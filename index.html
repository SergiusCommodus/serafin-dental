Meet your dentist

Dr. Caitlyn Serafin

DMD · Founder & Lead Dentist

Dr. Serafin earned her Bachelor of Science in Biochemistry and Molecular Biology from Penn State in 2021, building a deep scientific foundation in how the body works at the cellular level. She then trained at Temple University's Maurice H. Kornberg School of Dentistry in Philadelphia, one of the oldest dental schools in the United States. Her clinical years there were spent chairside, treating patients from routine cleanings to complex restorations.

She practices careful, conservative dentistry. She shows you your X rays, explains what she sees and lays out every option before any treatment begins. Anxious patients are always welcome in her chair.

"Great dentistry is precise work done with a gentle hand. My patients should always know why, not just what."

Our story

A family name on the door, and a family promise inside.

It started on the chapel steps at Temple, the day Caitlyn put on her white coat with her family around her. That coat marked her move from the classroom into the clinic, where she learned that the best care is both exact and kind.

Serafin Dentistry is built on that lesson. We take time to know our patients, we keep our schedule honest, and we never recommend work you do not need. From a child's first cleaning to a full smile restoration, we are here for every stage.
